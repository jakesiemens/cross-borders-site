Common Questions About Giving

Will I still get a receipt if I donate by Interac e-Transfer?
Yes! Once your Interac e-Transfer deposits into our account, our system automatically emails your Cross Borders Ministries donation receipt (complete with a unique receipt number and printable PDF copy)—just like credit card donations. First-time e-Transfer donors just share their name, email, and mailing address once using the form above.
How does giving by e-Transfer help Cross Borders Ministries?
Online credit card donations typically lose around 5–6% in combined platform and card processing fees. Interac e-Transfer carries zero fees for the ministry, which means 100% of your gift goes straight toward Bibles, orphan care, refugees, and discipleship on the field.
Can I set up monthly giving or donate from outside Canada?
Absolutely. For donors outside Canada or anyone who prefers automatic credit card billing, simply select the Credit Card / Monthly tab above to give securely through Donorbox. If you bank in Canada and want zero-fee monthly giving, most Canadian banking apps also let you schedule recurring Interac e-Transfers to info@cbministries.ca.
