About Us

Our Mission

Cross Borders Ministries exists to reach the unreached. Our heart is to travel deep into restricted and remote areas across Southeast Asia, boldly delivering the message of Christ to tribes and villages where the Gospel has never been heard.

Driven by the pressing need physically and spiritually, we operate out of our headquarters in Thailand. Over the years, we have seen God's incredible hand move to establish safe houses for orphans, plant deep seeds of discipleship, resupply those experiencing sudden disaster, and spark movements of faith in the high mountains.

The BORDERS Vision

We approach ministry holistically. Our seven pillars under the acronym BORDERS guide our daily work and our future prayers:

B

Bible Distribution

Getting Bibles to those who need them the most.

O

Orphan Care

Caring for the fatherless, as God commanded us.

R

Refugees

Helping refugees both physically and spiritually.

D

Discipleship

Helping Local Church leaders grow into Men and Women of God.

E

Evangelism

Partnering with local missionaries in gospel outreach.

R

Emergency Relief

Our goal to respond to crises as the hands and feet of Christ.

S

Schooling

A vision to supply books and education for those without access.

As you explore the stories from the field, we ask for your prayers. We are constantly seeking God’s guidance as we cross borders to further His Kingdom.

Reaching the unreached across every border. Dedicated to the BORDERS pillars in Southeast Asia.
