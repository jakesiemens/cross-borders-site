Our Seven Pillars

The BORDERS acronym represents the comprehensive, holistic approach we take to ministry. Some pillars represent our current active labor on the field, while others represent our deep prayer and vision for the future of this ministry.

B

Bible Distribution Active Ministry

Delivering the written Word of God to unreached and restricted people groups in their native languages.

Read More About Bible Distribution →
O

Orphans Active Ministry

Providing safe housing, nutritious food, and loving care to children who have lost their families.

Read More About Orphans →
R

Refugees Active Ministry

We are actively meeting displaced families at border crossing points with emergency relief, trauma counseling, and the hope of Christ.

Read More About Refugees →
D

Discipleship Active Ministry

We build deep spiritual foundations, training local leaders to pastor their own communities.

Read More About Discipleship →
E

Evangelism Active Ministry

Deploying dedicated teams to boldly and compassionately share the Gospel message where the name of Jesus is unknown.

Read More About Evangelism →
R

Relief Future Vision

Praying for the future capacity to respond to natural disasters and sudden crises with immediate food, medical supplies, and shelter.

Read More About Our Relief Vision →
S

Schooling Future Vision

A goal to break the cycle of poverty by providing education and vocational training to marginalized youth.

Read More About Our Schooling Vision →
